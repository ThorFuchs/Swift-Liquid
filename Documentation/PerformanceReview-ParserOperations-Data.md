M marks an absolute median change of at least 10%; S marks a six-run range of at least 10% on either side. Ranges are `(max − min) / median`; MAD is median absolute deviation divided by median. Three block effects are retained in order. A flag is a review prompt, not a causal verdict, speedup claim or release clearance. Displayed numbers are rounded; flags use unrounded values. Raw sample arrays and six unrounded run medians remain in the bound artifact.
